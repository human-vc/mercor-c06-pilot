Based on a review of the three Complaints and the Texas Rules of Civil Procedure (TRCP), here is the determination:

---

### 1. When the Original Complaint Was Served
* **The complaints do not state when the Original Complaint was served.** 
* Under **TRCP 22, 99, 106, and 107**, an original lawsuit is commenced by filing a petition/complaint, which is then served on the defendant along with a citation by an authorized process server or officer. Proof of service is provided by the officer’s or server’s return of citation filed with the court (TRCP 107). 
* Furthermore, **TRCP 21a(a)** expressly excludes *"the citation to be served upon the filing of a cause of action"* from standard service certificates.
* The face of Plaintiff’s Original Complaint reflects only its filing date—**(Filed: May 28, 2025)**—and includes a prayer requesting that Magnolia Gardens *"be cited to appear and answer."* Neither the Original Complaint, the First Amended Complaint (filed July 28, 2025), nor the Second Amended Complaint (filed February 14, 2026) states the date on which citation and the Original Complaint were actually served on Magnolia Gardens.

---

### 2. Last Day on Which the Motion to Dismiss Can Be Timely Filed Under TRCP 91a.3

#### A. Controlling Rule and Pleading:
* **TRCP 91a.3(a)** specifies:
  > *"A motion to dismiss must be: (a) filed within 60 days after the **first pleading containing the challenged cause of action** is served on the movant..."*
* Magnolia Gardens seeks to dismiss the **breach of contract** allegations.
* Review of the three complaints confirms that the breach of contract cause of action was first asserted in the **Plaintiff's Original Complaint** (Count V, paras. 10–14).
* Under Texas law, filing an amended petition asserting the same cause of action does **not** restart the 60-day clock under TRCP 91a.3(a) for causes of action that were already pleaded in the earlier petition (*see, e.g.*, *In re Butt*, 495 S.W.3d 455, 462–63 (Tex. App.—Corpus Christi 2016, orig. proceeding); *San Jacinto River Auth. v. Lewis*, 627 S.W.3d 775, 784–85 (Tex. App.—Houston [14th Dist.] 2021, pet. denied)). Therefore, the 60-day deadline is measured strictly from the service of the **Original Complaint**, not from the First or Second Amended Complaints.

#### B. Calculation of the Deadline:
1. **Formula under TRCP 91a.3(a) & TRCP 4:**
   * The motion must be filed within **60 days** after service of the Original Complaint.
   * Under **TRCP 4** (Computation of Time), the day of service is excluded. If the 60th day falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, the period extends to the end of the next day that is not a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday.

2. **Application Assuming Service on the Date of Filing (May 28, 2025):**
   * **Day 0:** May 28, 2025
   * **May:** 3 days remaining (May 29–31)
   * **June:** 30 days (total 33 days)
   * **July:** 27 days (reaches 60 days on **July 27, 2025**)
   * **Day of the week:** July 27, 2025 was a **Sunday**.
   * Pursuant to **TRCP 4**, the deadline rolled over to the next business day: **Monday, July 28, 2025**.
   *(Note: July 28, 2025 is the exact date Susan Whitaker filed her First Amended Complaint).*

3. **Conclusion on Timeliness:**
   * If service occurred on or about the filing date (May 28, 2025), the last day to file a timely TRCP 91a Motion to Dismiss the breach of contract claim was **July 28, 2025**. That deadline has long expired, and the motion is now **time-barred**.
   * Strictly within the four corners of the complaints, because the exact return/service date is omitted, the deadline is **60 days after the date of actual service** of the Original Complaint (adjusted under TRCP 4 if the 60th day falls on a weekend/holiday).

---

### 3. Summary for Alternative Scenarios
Even if an argument were made that an amended pleading restarted the clock (which is contrary to TRCP 91a.3(a) for pre-existing claims):
* **From First Amended Complaint (filed July 28, 2025):** 60 days was **Friday, September 26, 2025** (already passed).
* **From Second Amended Complaint (face date: February 14, 2026):** 60 days would be **Wednesday, April 15, 2026** *(or February 2, 2026 if using the file-naming date of December 2, 2025)*. However, as noted above, under TRCP 91a.3(a), an amended pleading does not revive an expired deadline for an existing breach of contract cause of action.
