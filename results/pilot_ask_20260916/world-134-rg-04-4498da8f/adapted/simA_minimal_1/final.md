### **Primary Case: Relative Share Loss (% of TrainIQ's Market Share)**
*In equity research and market share dynamics, a competitor losing a percentage of its market share denotes a relative reduction of its existing share (e.g., losing 7% of its $78.77M revenue / 18.62% share).*

*Calculations:*

* **Firm C (18% relative loss):** 
  * Share gained: $18\% \times 18.6218\% = 3.3519\%$
  * New Share: $11.7509\% + 3.3519\% = \mathbf{15.10\%}$
