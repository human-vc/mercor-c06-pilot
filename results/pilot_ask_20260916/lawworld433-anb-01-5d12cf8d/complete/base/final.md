### **2. Last Day to Timely File the Motion to Dismiss under TRCP 91a.3**

#### **Primary Legal Conclusion: The Deadline Has Expired (August 6 or August 8, 2025)**
Under **Texas Rule of Civil Procedure 91a.3(a)**, a motion to dismiss must be:
> *"filed within 60 days after the **first pleading containing the challenged cause of action** is served on the movant."*

* **The Challenged Cause of Action:** Magnolia Gardens seeks to dismiss the **breach of contract** allegations. 
* **The First Pleading:** Breach of contract was asserted in the **Original Complaint** filed May 28, 2025. Both subsequent amended complaints continue to assert that exact same cause of action (*First Amended Complaint*, § I: *"Plaintiff continues to assert a single cause of action for breach of contract"*; *Second Amended Complaint*, § I: *"Plaintiff continues to bring a single cause of action for breach of contract"*).
* Under Texas law interpreting TRCP 91a.3(a), the 60-day clock begins when the defendant is served with the *first* pleading asserting the cause of action. Subsequent amended pleadings do **not** restart or reset the 60-day deadline for a cause of action that was already pleaded in an earlier petition.
* **Computation under TRCP Rule 4:**
  * If served on **June 7, 2025**: The 60-day deadline expired on **Wednesday, August 6, 2025**.
  * If served on **June 9, 2025**: The 60-day deadline expired on **Friday, August 8, 2025**.

Therefore, the deadline to timely file a Rule 91a Motion to Dismiss challenging the breach of contract cause of action **has already passed**.
