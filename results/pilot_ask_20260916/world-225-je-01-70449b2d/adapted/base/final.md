### **Step-by-Step Valuation & Inflation Methodology**

#### **1. Service Revenue Proportion (2024A Baseline)**
From the historical financials (`Historicals` sheet):
* **2024A Total Revenue:** $5,420.0 million
* **2024A Service Revenue:** $895.0 million
* **Service Revenue Proportion:**  
  $$\text{Proportion}_{\text{Service}} = \frac{\$895.0\text{M}}{\$5,420.0\text{M}} \approx 16.5129\%$$
* **Non-Service Revenue Proportion:**  
  $$\text{Proportion}_{\text{Non-Service}} = 1 - 16.5129\% \approx 83.4871\% \quad \left(\frac{\$4,525.0\text{M}}{\$5,420.0\text{M}}\right)$$

---

#### **2. 2025 Forecast Operational Expenses & Inflation Impact**
From the REIT projections (`Projections (C-Corp)` & `REIT Conversion` sheets):
* **2025E Forecast Revenue:** $5,826.500 million
* **2025E Base EBITDA:** $2,505.395 million ($2,504.0 million in FCF schedule)
* **2025E Base Operating Expenses (excl. D&A):**  
  $$\text{Opex}_{2025\text{E}} = \text{Revenue} - \text{EBITDA} = \$5,826.500\text{M} - \$2,505.395\text{M} = \$3,321.105\text{ million}$$

Applying the **2% inflation rate**:
* **Total Operating Expense Increase:**  
  $$\Delta \text{Opex}_{\text{Total}} = \$3,321.105\text{M} \times 2\% = \$66.4221\text{ million}$$
* **Service Opex Increase (Passed On):**  
  $$\Delta \text{Opex}_{\text{Service}} = \$66.4221\text{M} \times 16.5129\% = \$10.9682\text{ million}$$
  *(Fully offset by higher Service revenues; net EBITDA impact = $0)*
* **Non-Service Opex Increase (Unpassed / Absorbed):**  
  $$\Delta \text{Opex}_{\text{Non-Service}} = \$66.4221\text{M} \times 83.4871\% = \$55.4539\text{ million}$$

---

#### **3. Inflation-Adjusted 2025E EBITDA**
$$\text{EBITDA}_{\text{adj}} = \$2,505.395\text{M} - \$55.4539\text{M} = \mathbf{\$2,449.9411\text{ million}}$$
*(If calculated from $2,504.000M base EBITDA: unpassed cost = $55.4772M, yielding adjusted EBITDA = $2,448.5228M)*
