## Answer

**Short version:** The Rule 91a.3(a) clock runs from service of the **Original Complaint (filed and served May 28, 2025)**, not from either amended pleading. Sixty days after May 28, 2025 is **Sunday, July 27, 2025**, which under Rule 4 rolls to the next non-weekend day — so the last day Magnolia could have timely filed its Rule 91a motion to dismiss the breach-of-contract cause of action was **Monday, July 28, 2025** (or **Wednesday, July 30, 2025** if the complaint was served on Magnolia by mail, which adds three days under Rule 21a(c)). That window has long since closed.

### How I got there

**1. Which pleading starts the clock — Rule 91a.3(a)**
Rule 91a.3(a) requires the motion to be "filed within 60 days after the **first pleading containing the challenged cause of action** is served on the movant." The challenged cause of action is breach of contract, and it first appears in the **Original Complaint for Breach of Contract**, filed May 28, 2025 (Cause No. D-1-GN-25-004732, 53rd Judicial District, Travis County). The First Amended Complaint (July 28, 2025) and Second Amended Complaint expressly state that Plaintiff "continues to bring a single cause of action for breach of contract" — they re-plead the *same* cause of action, so they are not the "first pleading containing" it and do **not** restart the 60-day period.

**2. Amendments don't extend the deadline — Rule 91a.5**
Rule 91a.5(b) contemplates only that, where the respondent amends the challenged cause of action at least 3 days before a hearing on a **pending** motion, the *movant* may file an amended motion directed at the amended cause of action; Rule 91a.5(d) restarts the time periods **only for that amended motion**. There is no provision that reopens or re-runs the 60-day filing window in 91a.3(a) simply because the plaintiff amended. (Rule 91a.5(c) also bars the court from considering an amendment not filed as of the hearing date.) So neither the July 28 FAC nor the SAC gave Magnolia a fresh 60 days.

**3. Date of service of the Original Complaint**
The only date stated on the Original Complaint is its filing date, **May 28, 2025**. Under Rules 21 and 21a a filed pleading is served on the parties' attorneys of record at the time of filing (electronically through the e-filing manager, where service is complete on transmission), so service on Magnolia is properly treated as occurring **May 28, 2025** — the same day it was filed. (Caveat: none of the three complaints contains a constable's return of service or a separate service date. If the actual citation/return of service shows a later date, the deadline is 60 days after that date, plus 3 days if service was by mail under Rule 21a(c).)

**4. Computation — Rule 4**
Exclude the triggering day (May 28); count forward 60 days → **July 27, 2025**, a Sunday; Rule 4 extends the period to the end of the next day that is not a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday → **Monday, July 28, 2025**. (Mail-service variant: July 27 + 3 = **July 30, 2025**, a Wednesday.) A document e-filed by midnight in the court's time zone on that day is timely (Rule 21(f)(5)).

**5. Related deadlines had the motion been filed on time — Rule 91a.3(b)–(c), 91a.4, 91a.6**
Filed July 28, 2025 → hearing no earlier than **August 18, 2025** (21 days); response due no later than 7 days before the hearing; ruling required within 45 days, i.e., by **September 11, 2025**; each party entitled to at least 14 days' notice of hearing.

### Practical consequence
A Rule 91a motion aimed at the breach-of-contract allegations, if filed now (after the Second Amended Complaint), is **untimely under Rule 91a.3(a)** — the 60-day period expired July 28, 2025 (July 30 if by mail). Magnolia's remaining avenues are the cumulative procedures preserved by Rule 91a.9 (e.g., special exceptions under Rule 91, motion to strike, or a traditional no-evidence/other dismissal motion), plus Rule 5 enlargement for cause — but not Rule 91a.

### Two discrepancies in the file you should be aware of (they do not change the answer)
- The Second Amended Complaint's **filename says December 2, 2025**, but the **document itself reads "Filed: February 14, 2026."** Even if one mistakenly measured 60 days from the SAC, the dates would be Feb. 2, 2026 (from Dec. 2, 2025) or Apr. 15, 2026 (from Feb. 14, 2026) — but that measurement is wrong under 91a.3(a) because the SAC is not the first pleading containing the breach-of-contract cause of action.
- The Original and First Amended Complaints plead Magnolia as an **Illinois** corporation; the Second Amended Complaint pleads a **Delaware** corporation. That affects later Rules 120a/121 analysis, not the 91a.3 computation.

**Bottom line:** Original Complaint served **May 28, 2025**; last day to timely file the Rule 91a motion to dismiss the breach-of-contract claim: **Monday, July 28, 2025** (July 30, 2025 if service was by mail). No deliverable files were created — the answer is provided above.
