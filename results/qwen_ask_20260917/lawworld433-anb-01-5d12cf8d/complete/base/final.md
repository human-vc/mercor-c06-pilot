I reviewed all three complaints and Rule 91a (with Rules 4 and 21a) in the Texas Rules of Civil Procedure (Aug. 31, 2025 edition). Here is the analysis and answer.

## The governing text

**Rule 91a.3(a):** a motion to dismiss "must be **filed within 60 days after the first pleading containing the challenged cause of action is served on the movant**."

The trigger is the **first** pleading containing the challenged cause of action — not the most recent pleading. All three complaints plead a **single, identical cause of action for breach of contract** (Original Complaint § V ¶¶ 10–14; First Amended Complaint § V ¶¶ 10–14; Second Amended Complaint § V ¶¶ 13–15). The amendments add/refine factual allegations and contractual provisions, but they do not add a new cause of action. So the 60-day clock is measured from service of the **Original Complaint**, and the later amendments do not restart it. (The clock restarts only under Rule 91a.5(b)/(d), which presupposes a 91a motion already on file when the challenged cause of action is amended — not the situation here.)

## The dates

| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Original Complaint filed | **May 28, 2025** (per the filing notation) |
| Served on Magnolia Gardens (filing + 10 days) | **Saturday, June 7, 2025** |
| 60-day Rule 91a.3(a) window (Rule 4: day of service not counted; counting begins June 8) | **Wednesday, August 6, 2025** |

**Last day to timely file the Rule 91a motion: Wednesday, August 6, 2025.** August 6, 2025 is not a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, so Rule 4 provides no extension.

For reference, the other pleadings (each served 10 days after filing) would have produced *later* dates if the clock ran from the last pleading — First Amended Complaint filed July 28, 2025 / served August 7, 2025 / 60 days = October 6, 2025; Second Amended Complaint (notated "Filed: February 14, 2026" on its face, though the file name reads 2025.12.02) / served February 24, 2026 / 60 days = April 25, 2026, a Saturday, extending to Monday, April 27, 2026. Those dates are not controlling under 91a.3(a); the earliest date — August 6, 2025 — is.

## Practical consequence

Because the Second Amended Complaint was filed well after August 6, 2025, a **first** Rule 91a motion directed at the breach-of-contract claim is now outside the 60-day window and would be untimely under Rule 91a.3(a). Magnolia's remaining options are a Rule 91a motion by agreement/leave, or dismissal under other procedures expressly preserved by Rule 91a.9 (e.g., special exceptions under Rules 90–91, plea in intervention/other dispositive motions), rather than Rule 91a.

Also note the ancillary timing requirements if a motion is filed: it must be filed **at least 21 days before the hearing** (91a.3(b)), the court must rule **within 45 days of filing** (91a.3(c)), any response is due **7 days before the hearing** (91a.4), and each party is entitled to **at least 14 days' notice** of hearing (91a.6).
