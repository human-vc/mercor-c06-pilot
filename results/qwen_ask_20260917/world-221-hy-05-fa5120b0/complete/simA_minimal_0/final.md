## Reading the bridge
Roughly 10% of the value creation is "deal" value (synergies +4.64%, TPVG's NAV contribution +5.28%), while the remaining ~24% comes from the pro forma re-rating: on the TTM account the combined platform's earnings and revenue base (PF NII +55%, PF Sales +33%) is capitalized at the same median multiples, and it more than offsets the dilution from issuing 28.3M shares and paying the 25% premium. Note the P/S leg is by far the most aggressive of the three (BBDC trades at ~3.2x its own sales, not 9.11x), so the absolute level of the implied values is multiple-driven — the *relative* step percentages are the meaningful output, and they are unchanged if you use the 12-company comp set only for the medians in Steps 1–3.
