# Client Question: Whitaker v. Magnolia Gardens, Cause No. D-1-GN-25-004732

Under Tex. R. Civ. P. 91a.3(a), the motion to dismiss must be filed within 60 days after the first pleading containing the challenged cause of action is served on the movant. The first pleading containing the breach of contract cause of action is Plaintiff's Original Complaint. The three complaints state only their filing dates (Original Complaint: May 28, 2025; First Amended Complaint: July 28, 2025; Second Amended Complaint: February 14, 2026) and none of them states when the Original Complaint was served on Magnolia Gardens.

On what date was the citation and Plaintiff's Original Complaint served on Magnolia Gardens, Inc. (and by what method, for example personal delivery, certified mail, or service on its registered agent, or by a signed waiver or acceptance of service)?
