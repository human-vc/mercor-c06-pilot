**Answer**

**Original Complaint service date: June 7, 2025.** The Original Complaint bears a filing date of May 28, 2025 (Cause No. D-1-GN-25-004732, 53rd District Court, Travis County). Ten days after filing is Saturday, June 7, 2025.

**Last day to timely file the Rule 91a motion: Wednesday, August 6, 2025.** That deadline has already passed, so a Rule 91a motion directed at the breach of contract claim cannot now be timely filed.

**How I got there**

1. Rule 91a.3(a) (Aug. 31, 2025 TRCP compilation, p. 52) requires the motion to be "filed within 60 days after the first pleading containing the challenged cause of action is served on the movant." The clock is tied to the *first* pleading that contains the cause of action, not the most recent one.

2. All three complaints plead the same single cause of action, breach of the 2023 Residential Care Agreement. The Original Complaint (Section V, paras. 10-14) pleads it; the First Amended Complaint (filed July 28, 2025) and the Second Amended Complaint each state on their face that Plaintiff "continues to assert/bring a single cause of action for breach of contract." So the Original Complaint is the "first pleading containing the challenged cause of action."

3. Counting under Rule 4: the day of service (June 7) is excluded; day 1 is June 8 and day 60 is August 6, 2025. August 6, 2025 is a Wednesday and not a legal holiday, so there is no roll-forward.

4. The amended complaints do not restart the period. Rule 91a.5(d) restarts the rule's time periods only when the *movant* files an amended motion under 91a.5(b); an amended *pleading* is not a restarting event. Even on the most defendant-friendly reading (treating the new breach theories in the Second Amended Complaint, such as quarterly reassessments and 60-day notice, as separately challengeable), 60 days from service of that pleading has also run: February 24, 2026 service gives an April 25, 2026 (Saturday) end date, rolling to Monday, April 27, 2026, which is well before today (September 16, 2026).

**Two items to flag**

- The Second Amended Complaint's face says "Filed: February 14, 2026," but its filename is dated 2025.12.02. This discrepancy does not change the result: under the filename date the alternate deadline would have been February 10, 2026, also long past. You may want to confirm the true file-stamp date for the record.
- The task stated a service date but not a method. If the original citation were treated as served by mail and a three-day Rule 4/21a extension were applied, the deadline would move only to Monday, August 11, 2025, still passed.

**Practical note:** Rule 91a.9 makes the rule cumulative of other dismissal procedures, so Magnolia Gardens can still attack the pleading through special exceptions (Rule 91) or a summary judgment motion (Rule 166a); those vehicles are not subject to the 91a.3 60-day window. If the client nevertheless wants to file under 91a, it would need a Rule 5 enlargement for good cause, which is discretionary and uncertain.
