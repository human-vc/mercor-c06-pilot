**How it was computed.** Same mechanics as BU Assessment.xlsx and as Jesse described in the 11/20 check-in: per criterion, score = 5 − |Rating − Ideal|, summed across the five criteria. The v2 ideals were converted from the framework's descriptors using the 1–5 scale defined on the criterion tabs (Very low = 1, Low = 2, Moderate/Medium = 3, High = 4, Very high = 5; Very weak through Very strong for commercial viability). A range descriptor such as "Low to very low" is scored at the midpoint (1.5), which is why some cells carry half points.

**Robustness.** Since no file specifies how to collapse a range to one number, I re-ran the scoring under every alternative convention (lower end, upper end, any-value-in-range counts as a perfect match). Discontinue is the top scorer under all of them (totals 20 to 22), and Graduate is the runner-up under all but one. The recommendation does not depend on the convention; only the exact totals do.
