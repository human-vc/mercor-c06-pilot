## Answer

**Short answer: the last day to file a timely Rule 91a motion to dismiss the breach-of-contract claim is Wednesday, August 6, 2025. The Original Complaint was served on Magnolia Gardens on Saturday, June 7, 2025.**

### The controlling rule

Tex. R. Civ. P. **91a.3(a)**: a motion to dismiss "must be filed **within 60 days after the first pleading containing the challenged cause of action is served on the movant**." Two features of that text drive the result:

- The clock runs from **service**, not filing.
- The clock runs from the **first pleading containing the challenged cause of action** — not the most recent pleading.

### The dates from the three complaints

| Pleading | Filed | Served (filed + 10 days) |
|---|---|---|
| Original Complaint (breach of contract) | **May 28, 2025** | **June 7, 2025** |
| First Amended Complaint | July 28, 2025 | August 7, 2025 |
| Second Amended Complaint | February 14, 2026 (per the face of the document) | February 24, 2026 |

All three pleadings assert a **single cause of action for breach of contract** (each says so expressly: "Plaintiff continues to assert a single cause of action for breach of contract"). The amended complaints add factual allegations and re-list the alleged breaches, but they do not plead a new cause of action. The **first pleading containing the challenged cause of action is therefore the Original Complaint**, served **June 7, 2025**.

### Computation (Tex. R. Civ. P. 4)

- Exclude the day of the triggering event (service, June 7, 2025); begin counting June 8.
- Day 60 = **August 6, 2025**, which is a **Wednesday** — not a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, so no extension under Rule 4.

**Deadline: Wednesday, August 6, 2025.**

### Common traps to avoid

- **Do not run the 60 days from the First Amended Complaint** (that would give October 6, 2025) **or the Second Amended Complaint** (that would give Saturday, April 25, 2026 → Monday, April 27, 2026). Because breach of contract was already pleaded in the Original Complaint, later amendments of that same claim do not restart the 91a.3(a) window.
- **Rule 91a.3(b) and (c) are separate requirements**, not the filing deadline: the motion must also be filed at least 21 days before the hearing, and the court must rule within 45 days of filing.
- **Mail-service caveat:** the complaints do not state the method of service. If the Original Complaint was served **by mail** (rather than electronically or in person), Rule 21a(c) adds 3 days, making the deadline August 9, 2025 — a Saturday — which rolls under Rule 4 to **Monday, August 11, 2025**. Absent that showing, the deadline is August 6, 2025.
- **If Magnolia later wants to attack allegations appearing for the first time in the Second Amended Complaint** (e.g., failure to conduct quarterly reassessments, failure to deliver Care Plan services, failure to give 60 days' notice), the mechanism is an **amended motion** under Rule 91a.5(b), which under 91a.5(d) "restarts the time periods in this rule" — i.e., 60 days from service of that amendment — but that device does not revive an untimely motion aimed at the claim as originally pleaded.
