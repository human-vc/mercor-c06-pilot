## Answer

**Bottom line: the 60‑day window under Rule 91a.3(a) ran from service of the *Original* Complaint, and it closed on Monday, July 28, 2025. Neither amended pleading restarted it.**

---

### 1. The governing rule

**Tex. R. Civ. P. 91a.3(a):** a motion to dismiss "must be … **filed within 60 days after the first pleading containing the challenged cause of action is served on the movant**."

The trigger is the *first pleading containing the challenged cause of action* — not the most recent pleading, and not the pleading the movant happens to be responding to.

**Tex. R. Civ. P. 4 (computation of time):** the day of the event that starts the period is excluded; every intervening day is counted (weekends and holidays included for periods longer than five days); and "[t]he last day of the period … is to be included, **unless it is a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, in which event the period runs until the end of the next day which is not a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday**."

### 2. When the Original Complaint was served

The only date the Original Complaint itself carries is **May 28, 2025** ("(Filed: May 28, 2025)"), and the Travis County district‑court file copy reflects no later service date in any of the three complaints. On the face of the pleadings, therefore, the Original Complaint was **served on Magnolia Gardens on May 28, 2025**, and that is the date the 91a.3(a) clock began. (If a separate return of service/citation shows a later date of actual service, the deadline shifts to 60 days after *that* date, subject to the same Rule 4 roll‑forward; nothing in the three complaints shows a different date.)

### 3. The calculation

| Step | Result |
|---|---|
| Challenged cause of action | Breach of contract (the only cause of action pleaded in all three complaints) |
| First pleading containing it | **Original Complaint**, served **May 28, 2025** (Wed.) |
| Day 1 of the 60 days (Rule 4 excludes the trigger day) | May 29, 2025 |
| Day 60 | **July 27, 2025 — a Sunday** |
| Rule 4 roll‑forward to next non‑Saturday/Sunday/holiday | **Monday, July 28, 2025** |

**Last day to timely file: Monday, July 28, 2025** (electronically filed by midnight, Central time, in the court's time zone).

### 4. Why the two amended complaints do not buy more time

- **First Amended Complaint (filed July 28, 2025)** — pleaded the *same single cause of action for breach of contract*, re‑alleging the same three contractual provisions (8:1 staffing, three daily meals, daily programming) and the same February 2025 corporate directives. It was filed on the very day the 91a.3(a) window closed; it does not create a new "first pleading containing the challenged cause of action."
- **Second Amended Complaint (dated "Filed: February 14, 2026" in the document; the file name is 12/02/2025)** — expressly states Plaintiff "continues to bring a **single cause of action for breach of contract**," merely "refin[ing] and clarif[ying]" factual allegations. Additional breach allegations (missed quarterly reassessments, care‑plan services, the 60‑day notice clause) are new *facts/modes of breach* within the same cause of action, not a new cause of action, so they still trace back to the Original Complaint for 91a.3(a) purposes.
- **Rule 91a.5(b)–(d)** is the only mechanism that resets the clock, and it presupposes a **timely filed motion** that is then amended in response to an amendment made at least three days before the hearing. Because no timely motion existed within the window ending July 28, 2025, there is nothing to amend, and 91a.5(d)'s "restarts the time periods" provision is unavailable.
- Note also 91a.5(c): in ruling on a motion the court "must not consider a … amendment not filed as permitted by paragraphs (a) or (b)" — further confirmation that the rule keys the filing deadline to the *first* pleading, not to later amendments.

### 5. Practical consequence

A Rule 91a motion directed at the breach‑of‑contract allegations is **time‑barred under 91a.3(a)** — the deadline passed on **July 28, 2025**, more than six months before the Second Amended Complaint was filed. Any such motion filed now would be subject to denial as untimely unless relief comes from something outside Rule 91a itself (e.g., a Rule 91a.3(a) argument premised on a later actual service date for the Original Complaint, a Rule 5 enlargement for cause shown, or the alternative dismissal procedures preserved by Rule 91a.9, such as special exceptions under Rules 90–91 or summary judgment under Rule 166a) — none of which changes the 91a.3 answer.

**Summary:** Original Complaint served **May 28, 2025**; 60th day = Sunday, July 27, 2025; **last day to file a timely Rule 91a motion to dismiss the breach‑of‑contract claims = Monday, July 28, 2025.**
